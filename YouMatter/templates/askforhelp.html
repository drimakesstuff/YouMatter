Mental Health Support

Understanding Mental Health

Mental health encompasses our emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It influences how we think, feel, and act, as well as how we handle stress, relate to others, and make choices.

Recognizing When to Seek Help

Everyone experiences difficult emotions at times. However, if feelings of sadness, anxiety, or hopelessness persist or interfere with daily life, it is important to seek support from a qualified professional.

Suicide Prevention and Support

Suicidal thoughts are a serious matter and should never be ignored. Early intervention can save lives. If you or someone you know is struggling, please reach out immediately.

  • Talk openly with trusted friends, family, or mentors.
  • Contact professional counselors or helpline services.
  • Remember: seeking help is a sign of strength.

Counseling Services

Colleges and institutes often provide confidential counseling services to support students’ mental health. Make use of these resources—they are here to help.

Remember

Taking care of your mental health is as important as your physical health. You are not alone — support is available, and there is hope.
